Public inventory published by NC ABC and the individual county boards, polled a few times a day and shown here as-is. Local boards refresh their own numbers only a couple of times a day, so treat every quantity as a picture rather than a live feed — a bottle listed here may have left the shelf hours ago, and allocated bottles can be pre-claimed by mixed-beverage accounts. This is a personal hobby tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by NC ABC or any county board. Your starred list is stored in this browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.
